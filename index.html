P1 · Done

Graphics Playground

Build an interactive canvas: primitives, colors, a triangle, moving objects, and mouse + keyboard input. Five small challenges show each idea on its own.

P2 · Done

WebGL Primitive Playground

Step into WebGL2: buffers, shaders, attributes, uniforms, seven primitive modes, animation, and mouse + keyboard input. Six challenges isolate each idea.

P3 · Done

Transformation Playground

Move shapes with matrices: translation, rotation, uniform and non-uniform scaling, transform order, pivot, parent and child, and orbit. All live WebGL2.

P4 · Done

Camera Playground

Fly around a 3D cube: view matrix, perspective plus orthographic projection, FOV, near and far presets, and depth test. Six challenges across five labs.

P5 · Done

Textured and Lit Cube Playground

Light up a 3D cube: normals, flat and smooth shading, ambient plus diffuse plus specular light, texture sampling, filtering and wrapping, and full light plus camera control. All live WebGL2.
