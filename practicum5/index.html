Practicum 5

Textured and Lit Cube Playground

Practicum 4 answered where an object is. This one answers how its surface looks. Every vertex now carries a position plus a normal plus a UV pair. The vertex shader moves all three into place. The fragment shader samples a texture and adds ambient plus diffuse plus specular light. Every panel below runs that full chain live.

The Stage

This is the main bench. One cube spins on its own while you move the light around it. The geometry never changes. Only light direction, camera position, and sampling settings change, and the HUD reports every value back each frame.

Arrow keys move the light on X and Y. W moves it closer and S pulls it back. I and J and K and L move the camera. U moves it closer and O pulls it back. A raises ambient and Z lowers it. Bracket keys change UV scale. Minus and Plus change shininess. Sliders mirror all nine values both ways and dropdowns mirror shading plus filtering plus wrapping plus mipmap. F flips shading and T flips filtering and G flips wrapping and V flips texture and M flips mipmap and N flips scale and B flips normal matrix and Y flips orbit and 1 and 2 and 3 flip light parts and 0 flips lighting and X flips normalize and R resets and Space pauses.

Live data

How it works

  • Position finds the surface. Normal faces the light. UV picks the texel.
  • Held keys move light and camera every frame with delta time.
  • Single press keys flip one setting each and never repeat.

Try this

  • Press F and watch face edges turn into a rounded glow.
  • Press 0 and compare flat texture against lit texture.
  • Press X and read how raw normals skew edge brightness.
  • Park the light, drag Cam X, and watch the shine walk alone.
  • Raise UV scale past 2 and press G to see wrapping change.

B

Scale and normal matrix

Stretching a cube with plain rotation math tilts its normals away from the surface. The inverse transpose matrix keeps them square. This lab shows both paths on the same stretched cube.

scale

How it works

  • Naive lighting looks skewed on stretched faces.

How it works

E

Mipmap filtering

Plain LINEAR samples one level. Mipmap modes blend levels by distance. This lab parks its camera far back so the gap between modes is easy to read on the small checker.

mipmap

Live data

How it works

  • The dropdown steps only this cube. The far cube calms under mipmaps.
  • The far cube shimmers under plain modes and calms under mipmaps.
  • Min filter carries the mode. Mag filter stays plain.
