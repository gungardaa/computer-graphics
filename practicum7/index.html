Practicum 7

Interactive 3D Prototype

Meeting 6 built a static Mini 3D Scene. This page turns it into an interactive application. One group shows parent and child hierarchy with live local and world readouts. One sphere shows roughness and metalness against a procedural environment. One imported GLB robot brings real hierarchy plus skinning plus fourteen animation clips. OrbitControls explores the scene while raycasting links the pointer to the objects, so hover and click each hold their own state. Every experiment below runs live in one stage.

The Stage

This is the full prototype from the module. A hierarchy demo group sits on the left with a body plus a head plus a small child box that tumbles. A lit green sphere in the middle carries live roughness and metalness. An imported GLB robot on the right runs its animation through an AnimationMixer with delta time while its whole hierarchy casts shadow. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right drag to pan. A plain click selects, a drag never does.

Focus the canvas then press P to swap the camera and E to flip the environment and Space to pause. Click selects and sets the Persistent scale. Dragging past 5 pixels keeps selection untouched. The Log button prints the child local position against its world position into the Console.

Live data

How it works

  • Local position lives on the child while world position composes every parent matrix above it.
  • One raycaster feeds both hover and click yet each keeps its own state variable.
  • Hover scale 1.06 is temporary and selected scale 1.12 persists until the next click.
  • scene.background stays a flat navy while scene.environment lights the PBR materials.

Try this

  • Turn Group rot Y and watch the child world row move while the child local row stays put.
  • Push Metalness to 1 then flip Env off to see a metal surface go dark.
  • Flip Recursive off and try to select the robot. Its child meshes fall out of reach.
  • Click the robot and switch Clip while it walks. The mixer crossfades without a reload.
A

Clip switchboard

One AnimationMixer drives many AnimationActions. Idle plus Walking plus Running swap with a short crossfade so the old action fades out while the new one fades in.

loading

Focus then press 1 or 2 or 3. Keys and buttons drive the same crossfade.

Live data

How it works

  • clipAction builds one action per clip and the mixer blends them.
  • fadeOut plus fadeIn overlap so the pose never snaps.
B

Click plays animation

Click the robot and it waves. The click selects through a raycast, then a one shot gesture clip plays and hands control back to the idle loop when it finishes.

loading

Click the robot or press the button. Either path fires the same gesture.

Live data

How it works

  • LoopOnce plus clampWhenFinished holds the last pose until idle returns.
  • The finished event is the hand back cue, not a timer.
C

Information panel

Click the cube or the sphere or the robot and read its live data. Name plus position plus rotation plus scale plus material type all come back through the same selectable root lookup the stage uses.

pick one

Click any object. Clicking empty space clears the panel.

Selected object

How it works

  • Child meshes inside the GLB map back to the labeled root before reporting.
  • Material type reads from the first mesh under the selected root.
D

Hierarchical solar system

Sun holds an orbit group that holds Earth that holds a moon orbit group that holds the Moon. No world position is written by hand. Every seat falls out of the hierarchy and one world readout per frame proves it.

orbit

Earth turns its orbit while the Moon turns a faster one inside it. One angle feeds each level.

Live data

How it works

  • Each orbit is a Group whose rotation carries every descendant.
  • getWorldPosition composes the chain while local values stay fixed.
E

PBR material gallery

Nine spheres in one grid. Rows hold roughness 0.1 plus 0.5 plus 0.9 against metalness 0 plus 0.5 plus 1 on the columns. The group turns slowly so reflections sweep across every surface.

rows R 0.9 0.5 0.1

Top row is roughest down to 0.1 at the bottom. Left column is metalness 0 moving right to 1.

Live data

How it works

  • Roughness blurs the reflection while metalness tints it with the base color.
  • Metal needs environment light. Without it the row of 1.0 goes dark.
F

Hover emissive

Hover a sphere and its material lights up through the emissive channel. Each sphere owns its own material so no shared asset is touched destructively.

hover

Move the pointer across the three spheres. The last touched material returns to its base color when the pointer leaves.

Live data

How it works

  • Base color is stored in userData before the first hover.
  • One material per sphere keeps the feedback local.
G

Selection rotation

Click any shape and it starts a slow spin timed by delta. Click another and the old spin stops where it stood while the new one takes over.

pick one

Click the cube or the sphere or the ring. Click empty space to stop every spin.

Live data

How it works

  • Selection holds one reference and the loop spins only that object.
  • Delta keeps the spin speed honest across displays.
H

Environment toggle

A polished metal sphere next to a matte one. Flip the environment and compare. The metal surface loses its reflection while the matte surface barely moves.

Environment ON

Focus then press E. The button mirrors the same flag.

Live data

How it works

  • scene.environment reads as a light source for every PBR material.
  • scene.background stays flat so only the materials change.
