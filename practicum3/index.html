Practicum 3

Transformation Playground

Practicum 3 is about moving shapes without touching their points. Every triangle below keeps the same local numbers. One small 3 by 3 matrix does all the moving and turning and stretching on the GPU.

The Stage

This is the main bench. The cyan triangle answers to you. The orange one moves on its own. Both share one triangle shape and only their matrices differ.

Click the canvas to place the cyan triangle there. Arrow keys slide it. Q and E turn it. Plus and minus stretch it evenly. Z and X stretch it wide. C and V stretch it tall. R resets. T flips the order. Keys 1 2 3 jump to presets. Space pauses.

Live data

How it works

  • Local points stay fixed in one buffer all the time.
  • Each frame builds a fresh matrix from position plus angle plus scale.
  • The matrix rides to the shader as one uniform per draw.

Controls

  • Continuous keys read every frame with delta time.
  • Single press keys reset and flip and pause.
  • Click writes position straight from the mouse.

LAB B

Door hinge

A door swings on its hinge, marked by the bright dot. The math shifts the hinge to the origin, turns, then shifts back.

hinge

Just watch. The panel swings around the bright dot.

Live data

How it works

  • Hinge offset moves to the origin first.
  • Rotation happens around the origin.
  • The offset moves back last of all.
LAB C

Family plus orbit

Two ideas in one view. The small mint triangle is a child of the cyan one and follows its parent everywhere. The orange triangle circles the middle by turning a shifted shape.

family

Just watch. The child rides along and the outer one loops.

Live data

How it works

  • Child world equals parent world times child local.
  • Orbit equals spin times shift times turn.
  • The middle sun only breathes and never moves.
LAB D

Matrix readout

This card shows the actual numbers behind the stage. Nine values decide where the cyan triangle lands. Open the console to see the same matrix as a table.

Live data

How it works

  • Three columns hold turn plus scale plus shift.
  • The last row stays fixed for flat shapes.
  • Degree values turn into radians before the math.

Try this

  • Press M to log the live matrix to the console.
  • Compare the readout before and after T.
  • Watch the middle column swing as you turn.
