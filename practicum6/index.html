Three.js Mini 3D Scene

Meetings 2 through 5 built 3D by hand with buffers plus shaders plus matrices. This page keeps every idea and hands the heavy lifting to Three.js as a thin layer above WebGL. One cube plus one sphere plus one ground plane share one light rig plus one shadow chain plus one delta driven loop. Everything below runs live.
